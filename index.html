Happy Girlfriend's Day ❤️

For My Thumpinu ❤️

Every heartbeat of mine whispers your name.
I have something written only for you...

Wishes may bloom or wither —
If it blooms,
Let mine be for her.
Make my girl the happiest,
Filled with peace and joy.

Bless her with all the love this world can give.
May her smile never fade,
Keep her safe in every season,
Healthy in body and soul.

Even in troubles, keep them away from her,
My love, my peace ❤️
That's all I wish for—
My sweetest being ❤️

Forever Yours ❤️
